## 3. Mathematical Formulations & Notation

### 3.1 Heterogeneous Particle Swarm Optimization (PSO)

The swarm consists of $N_{\text{agents}} = 9$ agents categorized into three functional modalities:
- **UAV (Unmanned Aerial Vehicles, $N=3$):** Velocity cap $v_{\max} = 1.8\text{ units/s}$, unrestricted elevation traversal.
- **USV (Unmanned Surface Vessels, $N=3$):** Velocity cap $v_{\max} = 1.0\text{ units/s}$, constrained to water/flooded terrain ($\text{elevation} \le 0$ or inundation depth $> 0.8\text{m}$).
- **Ground Rovers ($N=3$):** Velocity cap $v_{\max} = 0.7\text{ units/s}$, restricted to dry land ($\text{elevation} > 0$ and inundation depth $< 1.5\text{m}$).

At discrete time step $t$, agent $i$'s position $\mathbf{x}_i(t) = [x_i(t), y_i(t)]^T$ and velocity $\mathbf{v}_i(t) = [v_{x,i}(t), v_{y,i}(t)]^T$ are updated according to:

$$\mathbf{v}_i(t+1) = w \mathbf{v}_i(t) + c_1 r_1 (\mathbf{p}_i - \mathbf{x}_i(t)) + c_2 r_2 (\mathbf{g}_i(t) - \mathbf{x}_i(t))$$

$$\mathbf{x}_i(t+1) = \mathbf{x}_i(t) + \mathbf{v}_i(t+1)$$

Where:
- $w = 0.5$: Inertia weight balancing exploration and momentum.
- $c_1 = 1.2$: Cognitive learning parameter (pull toward personal best coordinate $\mathbf{p}_i$).
- $c_2 = 1.5$: Social learning parameter (pull toward local neighborhood best $\mathbf{g}_i(t)$).
- $r_1, r_2 \sim \mathcal{U}(0, 1)$: Uniform stochastic perturbation vectors.
- $\mathbf{g}_i(t)$: Neighborhood best target chosen from connected peers within communication range:

$$\mathcal{N}_i(t) = \{ j \in \mathcal{A} \setminus \{i\} \mid \|\mathbf{x}_i(t) - \mathbf{x}_j(t)\|_2 \le R_{\text{comm}}, \text{status}_j = \text{ACTIVE} \}$$

$$\mathbf{g}_i(t) = \arg\max_{j \in \mathcal{N}_i(t)} \big( |\mathcal{C}_j(t)| \big)$$

Where $|\mathcal{C}_j(t)|$ is the number of un-surveyed coastal grid cells explored by neighbor $j$. If $\mathcal{N}_i(t) = \emptyset$, $\mathbf{g}_i(t) = \mathbf{p}_i$.

### 3.2 Hazard-Aware A\* Path Planning

Path planning over the $50 \times 50$ bathymetric and inundation grid $\mathcal{G}$ determines collision-free, minimum-cost trajectories between agent starting locations and targets (or civilian locations and highland shelters).

The total evaluation function for node $n = (x, y)$ is:
$$f(n) = g(n) + h(n)$$

Where $h(n)$ is the Octile distance heuristic to goal $n_g = (x_g, y_g)$:
$$h(n) = D_1 (|x - x_g| + |y - y_g|) + (D_2 - 2 D_1) \min(|x - x_g|, |y - y_g|)$$
With straight-step cost $D_1 = 1.0$ and diagonal-step cost $D_2 = \sqrt{2} \approx 1.414$.

The edge traversal cost $c(n, n')$ from node $n$ to neighbor $n'$ incorporates physical bathymetric slope and inundation risk:

$$c(n, n') = \Delta d \cdot \Big[ 1.0 + \alpha \cdot \max(0, z(n') - z(n)) + \beta \cdot d_{\text{inund}}(n') + \gamma \cdot \mathcal{R}(n') \Big]$$

Subject to traversal feasibility:
$$c(n, n') = \begin{cases} \infty & \text{if } z(n') \le 0 \text{ and agent} \in \{\text{GROUND}, \text{CIVILIAN}\} \\ \infty & \text{if } d_{\text{inund}}(n') \ge 1.5\text{m} \text{ and agent} \in \{\text{GROUND}, \text{CIVILIAN}\} \\ \infty & \text{if } z(n') > 0 \text{ and } d_{\text{inund}}(n') < 0.8\text{m} \text{ and agent} = \text{USV} \\ \text{finite} & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}$$

Where:
- $\Delta d \in \{1.0, \sqrt{2}\}$: Step distance.
- $z(n')$: Terrain elevation at cell $n'$ (meters).
- $d_{\text{inund}}(n')$: Estimated inundation water depth (meters).
- $\mathcal{R}(n') \in [0, 1]$: Categorical risk score.
- $\alpha = 0.02, \beta = 10.0, \gamma = 5.0$: Cost weighting hyperparameters.

### 3.3 Tsunami Surge & Inundation Propagation Model

The physical wave propagation model combines linear shallow-water phase velocity, Abe's empirical source displacement scaling, and Green's Law coastal shoaling:

1. **Phase Velocity in Deep Ocean:**
   $$v_{\text{deep}} = \sqrt{g \cdot h_{\text{ocean}}} = \sqrt{9.81 \cdot 4000} \approx 198.1\text{ m/s} \approx 713\text{ km/h}$$

2. **Abe's Source Displacement Scaling ($H_0$):**
   $$\log_{10}(H_0) = 0.52 M_w - 3.4 \implies H_0 = 10^{0.52 M_w - 3.4}$$
   For $M_w = 7.8$, $H_0 \approx 4.53\text{ meters}$.

3. **Green's Law Coastal Shoaling Factor:**
   $$K_s = \left( \frac{h_{\text{ocean}}}{h_{\text{coast}}} \right)^{0.25} = \left( \frac{4000}{8} \right)^{0.25} \approx 4.73$$

4. **Wave Arrival Time at Coastline Distance $D$:**
   $$t_{\text{arrival}} = \frac{D}{v_{\text{deep}}}$$

5. **Inundation Depth Decay over Coastal Topography ($x \ge x_{\text{coast}}$):**
   $$d_{\text{inund}}(x, y, t) = \max\Big(0, H_{\text{surge}}(t) - 0.35 \cdot z(x, y) - 0.05 \cdot (x - x_{\text{coast}})\Big)$$
